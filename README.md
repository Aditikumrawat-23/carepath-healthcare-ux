# CarePath — Healthcare Product Design

A conceptual mobile healthcare experience designed to help patients navigate their healthcare journey, from discovering a doctor to accessing prescription information and managing health records.

## Project Overview

CarePath explores how a more connected and understandable healthcare experience could help people manage key activities before, during, and after a consultation.

## Problem Statement

Managing healthcare can involve multiple activities, including finding a suitable doctor, arranging appointments, understanding appointment status, and accessing post-consultation information.

When these activities are spread across different services or rely on manual coordination, managing the overall healthcare journey can become difficult.

CarePath explores a patient-focused interface that brings these activities together in one organized experience.

## Project Goal

To design a simple, organized mobile experience that helps users navigate important healthcare activities, from doctor discovery and appointment booking to post-consultation information and health records.

## Key Features

- **Doctor Discovery:** Explore doctors and review relevant profile information.
- **Appointment Booking:** Select appointment dates and available time slots.
- **Appointment Confirmation:** Review appointment details after the booking flow.
- **Appointment Status:** View appointment progress and relevant next steps.
- **Prescription and Follow-Up:** Access organized prescription information and follow-up guidance.
- **Appointments and Health Records:** Explore a central location for appointments and health information.
- **Consistent Navigation:** Move between key sections through a unified interface.

## Design Process

The project followed a structured UX/UI design process:

1. Research and Insights
2. Product Strategy
3. UX Architecture
4. Low-Fidelity Wireframes
5. Design System
6. High-Fidelity UI Design
7. Interactive Prototyping
8. Improvement Opportunities and Future Iterations

## Design Approach

The visual direction uses a soft lavender and white palette, purple accents, rounded cards, and a clear typographic hierarchy.

The interface was designed to maintain consistency across screens while keeping important healthcare information and actions easy to locate.

## Project Status and Limitations

The seven high-fidelity screens and interactive prototype are complete. Usability testing has not yet been conducted, so the design's effectiveness and user outcomes remain unvalidated.

CarePath is a conceptual design project. It does not provide real appointment booking, backend services, medical-record integrations, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Future Scope

- Conduct usability testing with target users.
- Refine the interface based on observed user difficulties and feedback.
- Evaluate accessibility, readability, and navigation.
- Explore consent-based caregiver access.
- Investigate secure integrations with verified healthcare services.
